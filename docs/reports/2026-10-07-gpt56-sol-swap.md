## Reasoning-Knob Decision

**`reasoning_effort: "none"`.** It is the lowest documented level, and the frozen dose rule calls for minimum reasoning on every arm.

- It bills 0 reasoning tokens on every call here (0 across 80 probe calls and 3 stage-1 calls).
- It keeps temperature 0.0 and `top_p` legal, so `omit_sampling` is not needed.
- No exception has to be recorded, unlike GLM's forced-thinking exception. The arm also needs no cap-proofing: it keeps the shared `max_tokens` of 2048, sent as `max_completion_tokens`, as on gpt-5.1.

### Reading

- **v1 primitives are the strongest the gpt line has recorded:** 60/60, against gpt-5.1's 54/60. The gpt-5.1 losses were in count and left_right.
- **On dynamics, conflict_prediction 13/20 is a false-alarm profile.**
  - Every GT-yes item is right, but only 3/10 GT-no items are, and the model answered "yes" on 17/20 items.
  - That is gpt-5.1's shape (yes 15/20, GT-no 5/10), shifted further toward "yes", one item short of the registered degenerate threshold.
  - The 7 misses are all false "yes" answers. 5 of them carry letter A, but "yes" is option A on 13/20 items, so this is a yes-bias, not a letter prior.
  - None of the McNemar contrasts is significant. Against gpt-5.1 the split is 4/2 (p = 0.69); against gpt-4o, 1/5 (p = 0.22).
  - For noise scale: gpt-5.1's same-day retest moved 15 → 16 at temperature 0 (dose pilot). n = 20, single run.
- **Stage-1 behaviour is clean.** Both standoffs got the correct yielder with a plan that executes verbatim (no swap repair), and the re-check produced no false trigger. There were no refusals: gpt-4o's frame-locked refusal profile does not carry over.

## Flags for the Coordinator

The spec amendment should record each of these.

1. **OpenAI recommends gpt-6-sol, not gpt-5.6-sol, as gpt-5.1's replacement.**
   - The deprecations entry dated 2026-10-01 reads "Apr 1, 2027 gpt-5.1 → gpt-6-sol".
   - The latest-model guide lists GPT-5.6 under "Previous models", and states that GPT-6 Sol supports `none`.
   - This round follows the owner's ruling and did not test gpt-6-sol. The amendment should say why 5.6-sol was chosen over the vendor-recommended successor.
2. **Rolling pin.**
   - No dated snapshot exists, the served model echoes the undated id, and `system_fingerprint` is null. So, unlike gpt-4o's dated echo, **a provider-side model change is not observable per call**.
   - The arm needs the Group-D rolling-pin caveat even though it sits in Group C. The generation pair is now dated pin → rolling pin.
3. **Pricing is promotional** ($4 / $20 "at least through November 21, 2026"). The price after the promotion is unstated, and collection after 2026-11-21 may cost more. GPT-5.5's list price is $5 / $30.
4. **Image tokenization changed between the pair's two generations.**
   - gpt-4o uses tiles; gpt-5.6-sol uses patches: `high` fits within 2048×2048 and 2,500 patches, with a 1.2× multiplier.
   - The wire `detail: "high"` is the same string, but it does not mean the same preprocessing. A 1280×960 frame is kept at full resolution (1,440 tokens) rather than tiled.
   - This is a confound for any "generation" reading of Group C.
5. **No dose exception is needed.** `none` exists, bills 0 reasoning tokens, and keeps temperature 0.0 legal. This contrasts with GLM's recorded forced-thinking exception.
6. **conflict_prediction is a false-alarm profile.** "Yes" on 17/20 is one item short of the registered degenerate threshold (≥ 18/20), and GT-no is 3/10. The amendment may want the profile check stated for this arm.
7. **Safeguard pauses.** GPT-5.6 may pause generation "for several seconds mid-stream" while its classifiers review the output. None was evident here (probe maximum 3.1 s; stage-1 at most about 7 s net of pacing), but the stage-2 8 s bound could feel it.
8. **New usage key.** `cache_write_tokens` is now recorded. Cost aggregators that read only `prompt_tokens` and `completion_tokens` will underprice cache writes (1.25×). Every call here had 0 cache writes.

**Not done, by brief:** no head-on episode on the VM, no Responses-API migration, and no gpt-6-sol test.
