## 7. Verdict

1. **The hand-off race is fixed.** The old precursor (a late robot_1 resume during the cross→release hand-off) fell from 9 / 10 to 0 / 5. Its Nav2 footprint (2–3 navigate goals and 1–2 controller goals per hand-off) fell to at most the one pre-hand-off goal, which the guard cancelled every time. In 2 runs that goal had reached the controller, and the guard's controller stop cancelled the live `FollowPath` (the m4 mechanism). robot_1 never moved during its hold. The guard caused no regression: the pull-off, gates, phase advances and arrival times are unchanged or faster.
2. **The release-lane remedy is lead-agnostic.** robot_3-led releases (3) and robot_4-led releases (2) all resolved 4 / 4. The held receder pulled to −x and the lead passed on +x at 0.402–0.499 m, above the scan-dropout band, with the floor silent and no contact for any pair.
3. **The −x rule survived its falsification test**, in the risky direction, 3 / 3.
4. **Closure: IB10 and Step 0 are CLOSED.** Both blockers from the remedy report are resolved: §9.1 (robot_3-led remedy validation) and §9.2 (the hand-off race). The three freeze numbers stand as 0.33 / −x / 0.20. The scale is small (n = 5 here, 8 remedy passes in all) and mock-only, so this is a validation, not a confirmatory estimate.

**Open, non-blocking:**

- **(a) The floor's role** (remedy report §9.3).
- **(b) The 0.32–0.35 m scan blind band's cause** (§9.4). Remedy passes now clear it by 0.05–0.18 m.
- **(c) A scorer order flag (pre-existing).** On robot_3-led runs (b4, R1, P1, P2), `coverage_scene_scores ib10` reports `executed_order_correct: false`. The reason is that it reads the recede order from `plan.legs`, which keeps the mock proposal [robot_4, robot_3]. M4 actually dispatched the phases in χ order (`dispatch_order_source: chi`; first `reverse` = χ rank 0 in every run). Rule whether the scorer should read the dispatched phase order before v3 IB10 cells are scored.
