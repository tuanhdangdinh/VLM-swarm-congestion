Box geometry: `blocker_west` is centred at (−1.85, 0), 0.30 × 0.30 m, so its faces sit at x ∈ [−2.0, −1.7] and y ∈ [−0.15, 0.15]. The TurtleBot3 burger footprint radius is about 0.1 m. GT reports the base-frame position, not the body outline. A base frame 0.038 m from the box surface is therefore not exact overlap arithmetic, but it is unambiguous evidence that the robot was pressed against, and grinding along, the box's north face. In s42 the robot crept west along the face from x −1.68 to −1.94 over about 190 s, at speeds of 0–0.03 m/s. The 14 "collision" hits in `nav2_r2.log` are `collision_monitor` lifecycle lines, not stop events.

**Reading.**

- On the merged tree, robot_2 chose the north side in both replicates and pressed against the box face for 170–190 s.
- On the pre-fix tree (one run) it chose the south side and passed with 0.128 m of clearance.
- n = 2 against n = 1, so the side choice is not yet attributed to A′+H. The press itself, a DWB local minimum against a box corner or face, is the same family as the loop-map corner wedge (`docs/reports/2026-10-05-follow-waypoints-validation.md`, root-cause step 3).
- **It is a lead for stage 5, not a result:** with `clearing: true` on the local costmap, this geometry still produces a long near-zero-progress dwell.
