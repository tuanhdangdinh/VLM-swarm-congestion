## 2026-11-15 Erratum — Contact Wording

Ground truth's two contact lists, `robot_static_contacts` and `robot_robot_contacts`, are hard-coded to `[]` in `isaac/vgu_sim/acceptance_logging.py:99-100`. No contact detector exists, so an empty list shows nothing. The sound channel is the logged minimum pairwise distance (`min_pairwise_distance_m` and the GT positions), a centre-to-centre robot-robot distance. Nothing in GT logs contact with static obstacles. Same caveat: `docs/reports/2026-09-25-overnight-findings.md` (Metric correction 2 and its Rev 11 note) and `docs/reports/2026-10-08-s1-pilot.md` (GT `robot_static_contacts`); full account in section 8.7 of `docs/reports/2026-11-15-demo-campaign-results.md`.

- **What the statements rested on.** The criterion was already measured correctly, as minimum GT centre-to-centre robot separation ("How the contact and escape criteria were measured"), and that section already says robot-static contact is not measured. Only the unqualified headline "0 contacts" and the criteria row "Zero contacts" read wider than that.
- **What remains supported.** Robot-robot only: minima 0.253 m (s42) and 0.258 m (s43). "Zero contacts PASS" stands for robot-robot contact.
- **Static obstacles.** The head-on cell has an `aisle_barrier` corridor and a declared static blocker (`blocker_west`, `head_on_asymmetric_w0p2_eopen_bshort_o0.yaml`); robot-static contact was not measured, so the gap is material here.

## Reasoning-Knob Decision

**`reasoning_effort: "none"`.** It is the lowest documented level, and the frozen dose rule calls for minimum reasoning on every arm.

- It bills 0 reasoning tokens on every call here (0 across 80 probe calls and 3 stage-1 calls).
- It keeps temperature 0.0 and `top_p` legal, so `omit_sampling` is not needed.
- No exception has to be recorded, unlike GLM's forced-thinking exception. The arm also needs no cap-proofing: it keeps the shared `max_tokens` of 2048, sent as `max_completion_tokens`, as on gpt-5.1.

## Flags for the Coordinator

The spec amendment should record each of these.

1. **OpenAI recommends gpt-6-sol, not gpt-5.6-sol, as gpt-5.1's replacement.**
   - The deprecations entry dated 2026-10-01 reads "Apr 1, 2027 gpt-5.1 → gpt-6-sol".
   - The latest-model guide lists GPT-5.6 under "Previous models", and states that GPT-6 Sol supports `none`.
   - This round follows the owner's ruling and did not test gpt-6-sol. The amendment should say why 5.6-sol was chosen over the vendor-recommended successor.
2. **Rolling pin.**
   - No dated snapshot exists, the served model echoes the undated id, and `system_fingerprint` is null. So, unlike gpt-4o's dated echo, **a provider-side model change is not observable per call**.
   - The arm needs the Group-D rolling-pin caveat even though it sits in Group C. The generation pair is now dated pin → rolling pin.
3. **Pricing is promotional** ($4 / $20 "at least through November 21, 2026"). The price after the promotion is unstated, and collection after 2026-11-21 may cost more. GPT-5.5's list price is $5 / $30.
4. **Image tokenization changed between the pair's two generations.**
   - gpt-4o uses tiles; gpt-5.6-sol uses patches: `high` fits within 2048×2048 and 2,500 patches, with a 1.2× multiplier.
   - The wire `detail: "high"` is the same string, but it does not mean the same preprocessing. A 1280×960 frame is kept at full resolution (1,440 tokens) rather than tiled.
   - This is a confound for any "generation" reading of Group C.
5. **No dose exception is needed.** `none` exists, bills 0 reasoning tokens, and keeps temperature 0.0 legal. This contrasts with GLM's recorded forced-thinking exception.
6. **conflict_prediction is a false-alarm profile.** "Yes" on 17/20 is one item short of the registered degenerate threshold (≥ 18/20), and GT-no is 3/10. The amendment may want the profile check stated for this arm.
7. **Safeguard pauses.** GPT-5.6 may pause generation "for several seconds mid-stream" while its classifiers review the output. None was evident here (probe maximum 3.1 s; stage-1 at most about 7 s net of pacing), but the stage-2 8 s bound could feel it.
8. **Group D needs a decision.** The nine-arm table lists gpt-5.1 in both Group C and Group D (churn-risk pilots), and the ruling covers only C. The amendment must state one of two things:
   - gpt-5.1 stays in D as a retired pilot (its data stays citable); or
   - gpt-5.6-sol inherits the D slot, which its rolling pin would justify.
9. **This validation is offline-equivalent, not the full registered standard.** The model-wiring standard includes two head-on VM episodes (seeds 42/43). This round ran the probes and three stage-1 replays on stored frames, with an offline provenance harness that reproduces 9/9 recorded plans. The amendment should not record the arm as meeting the full standard until those episodes run.
   - **Resolved in Rev 2:** both episodes passed. See [Live VM Episodes (2026-10-07)](#live-vm-episodes-2026-10-07).
10. **New usage key.** `cache_write_tokens` is now recorded. Cost aggregators that read only `prompt_tokens` and `completion_tokens` will underprice cache writes (1.25×). Every call here had 0 cache writes.

**Not done, by brief:** no head-on episode on the VM (done since, in Rev 2), no Responses-API migration, and no gpt-6-sol test.
