### Recommended Thesis Wording

1. "In a simulated four-robot intersection jam, the full coordination-layer pipeline (congestion trigger, VLM call, grounding and phased dispatch) cleared the jam in 4 of 5 episodes, against 0 of 5 with the coordination layer off (intersection run 1, 13 November 2026). In a separate run (intersection run 2, 14 November 2026), the live-VLM arm cleared it in 5 of 5 and a scripted arm that skipped the VLM call and ran the grounder's default plan in 4 of 4 valid episodes (5 of 5 after a post hoc re-run), in similar time (exploratory pilots, n = 5 per arm, not independent samples)." (rung a)
2. "Gemini Robotics-ER 2 returned a valid, parseable decision in all 55 analyses of the demonstration campaign, but its plan was accepted unchanged in 6 of 53 plan-channel analyses; in the intersection scene, the only one where this was computed, it changed the executed commands relative to the grounder's default once, by swapping the order of two near-equivalent back-out manoeuvres." (rungs b, d) [superseded 2026-11-15, Rev 8: "… in all 67 analyses of the demonstration takes, but its plan was accepted unchanged in 6 of 65 plan-channel analyses (5 when a dispatch that contradicted the plan is not counted as accepted); …", rest unchanged] [Rev 10: "… in all 69 analyses of the demonstration takes, but its plan was accepted unchanged in 6 of 65 plan-channel analyses of the first twelve takes (5 when …); the convoy rerun added 2 analyses, neither accepted unchanged; …"]
3. "In the two-robot head-on scenes, the executed yielder was made correct by the grounder's feasibility gate, which overturned 37 of 79 model choices, not by the model, whose choice never beat the better of two trivial baselines." (rung c)
4. "In pre-registered offline replays, no tested model met the pre-registered rule for choosing the yielder from the scene rather than from robot labels or listing order, and several cells could not be assessed." (rung e)
5. "Safety evidence is limited to robot-pair centre distance; one sustained robot-obstacle contact, caused by an escort path that had no obstacle check, was confirmed offline from ground-truth poses and the recording." (rung f) [Rev 8 addition: "Offline replays also show reverse manoeuvres driving robots into declared obstacles in October runs; the obstacle guards and the stall abort built in response are validated offline only, and their live validation is in progress."] [Rev 10, replacing the last clause: "… of the mechanisms built in response, the reverse obstacle guard and the stall abort were each validated live once in a scripted safety test, and the escort obstacle guard offline only."]
6. (Rev 8) "A two-rater classification of the five-robot failures named no dominant cause; most failure units in the registered pilot (12 of 18: all 10 with the coordination layer off, which lack a clock anchor, and 2 of 8 with it on) and many in the exploratory pilots (13 of 33) could not be attributed from the recorded evidence." (rung e)

**Wording to avoid:**

- "The VLM resolves congestion" or "the VLM cleared the jam" (rung a: the trigger plus the grounder's default did).
- "The VLM plans the manoeuvre" or "the VLM's plan was executed" without the near-tie and recede-timing caveat (rung d).
- "0 contacts", "no collisions" or "collision-free" (rung f: no detector; one sustained static contact).
- "The VLM reasons about the scene", "uses the image" or "chooses the feasible yielder" (rungs c, e).
- "The pipeline generalises to multi-robot congestion" (convoy and five-robot negatives).
- Any rate or percentage from the demo takes (`DC` Final Summary: n is 1 to 5 per scenario, nothing is a rate).
